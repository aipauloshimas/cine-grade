# The edit prompt: skeleton + preservation block

One dense paste-ready block, prose only, no headers inside the block. The
user uploads the raw video alone and pastes this. No reference still.

## Assembly order
1. **Opening** — `Edit Video 1 to match the following visual treatment.`
2. **Look in one sentence** — the film's signature named as light, never as
   set: "Apply the amber daylight look of a dust-covered desert wasteland",
   "Apply a nocturnal moonlit color grade", "Apply the iconic green-tinted
   digital-world color grade".
3. **8-axis treatment as prose** (see `eight-axis.md`) — written onto the
   REAL elements of the frame: what the sky, wall, table, window, floor,
   garments and skin each BECOME under the new light. Include the
   subject's progression if the clip has movement (silhouette → lit face).
4. **Preservation block** — verbatim, below.
5. **"ONLY change:" clause** — the last sentence, always.

## Preservation block (verbatim — never trimmed)
```
Preserve Video 1 exactly: 100% facial identity, facial features, skin texture, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing, expressions, gestures, movements, timing, camera position, framing, background, and environment. Do not alter, replace, or regenerate any physical elements. Naturally relight the original scene so the new lighting direction, intensity, color, shadows, highlights, and exposure feel physically realistic and integrated with the existing environment. Match the mood without simply applying a color tint or color filter. Keep the result photorealistic, consistent frame-to-frame, sharp, and natural. No face changes, beautification, artificial skin, warping, added objects, background changes, motion changes, or AI artifacts. ONLY change: lighting, exposure, color grading, contrast, shadows, highlights, atmosphere, and overall cinematic mood.
```
Tailor only the "Naturally relight" sentence when the light has an obvious
diegetic source in frame ("— the cool light clearly coming from the window
on the left").

## Vocabulary that keeps the edit honest
- Surfaces **become**: "the pale wall becomes a deep indigo-violet field",
  "the white table surface turns cold grey-green".
- Garments **fall into** tones: "the warm beanie falls into a deep muted tone
  under the blue light", "the white sneakers become dusty warm off-white".
  Never "recolor the hoodie to burgundy".
- Distant elements **dissolve**: "the city skyline dissolves further into
  the orange dust as a ghostly silhouette".
- Atmosphere is **in the air**, not an added object: "as if thick luminous
  dust hangs in the air" — never "add fog to the room".

## Three production-validated prompts

### Blade Runner 2049 — Vegas amber (wide open concrete plaza, subject walking toward a ground-level camera, overcast, low hazy horizon with a faint skyline)
```
Edit Video 1 to match the following visual treatment. Apply the amber daylight look of a dust-covered desert wasteland: flood the entire scene with a dense monochromatic amber-orange atmosphere, as if thick luminous orange dust hangs in the air. The overcast white sky becomes a deep saturated amber wall of glowing haze; the grey concrete ground becomes warm dusty amber-orange, its surface lines fading into the haze; the faint city skyline on the horizon stays visible but dissolves further into the orange dust as a ghostly silhouette. Atmospheric perspective is strong: the farther from camera, the brighter and hazier the amber glow, with the horizon almost dissolving into light. In the early wide framing he reads as a soft dark semi-silhouette inside the glowing haze, with gentle warm rim light on the edges of his beanie, shoulders and arms; as he walks closer his face emerges from the atmosphere into soft directionless amber light, skin in a warm amber register with natural texture. Keep overall saturation low outside the amber dominant — his black hoodie stays deep soft black, the white sneakers become dusty warm off-white. Push contrast moderately: dense soft blacks in the clothing, protected glowing highlights in the haze, filmic shoulder rolloff, no clipping. Add subtle atmospheric diffusion and mild bloom around the brightest areas of the haze, fine film grain, gentle vignetting. Preserve Video 1 exactly: 100% facial identity, facial features, skin texture, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing, expressions, gestures, movements, timing, camera position, framing, background, and environment. Do not alter, replace, or regenerate any physical elements. Naturally relight the original scene so the new lighting direction, intensity, color, shadows, highlights, and exposure feel physically realistic and integrated with the existing environment. Match the reference mood without simply applying an orange tint or color filter. Keep the result photorealistic, consistent frame-to-frame, sharp, and natural. No face changes, beautification, artificial skin, warping, added objects, background changes, motion changes, or AI artifacts. ONLY change: lighting, exposure, color grading, contrast, shadows, highlights, atmosphere, and overall cinematic mood.
```

### The Matrix — green interrogation room (high-angle over a plain table, sparse room, window with a city view in a corner)
```
Edit Video 1 to match the following visual treatment. His skin turns pale green-olive with drained reds — especially in the final close-up where his face fills the frame — and the warm beanie falls into a desaturated muted olive-brown under the green light. Push global contrast high: dense heavy blacks with a green edge in the hoodie and shadows, dimmed slightly greenish highlights, no pure white anywhere in the frame. Desaturate all colors except the green dominant. Cold, clinical, artificial interrogation-room atmosphere; crisp and sharp, no diffusion, no warmth anywhere. Add fine film grain and a subtle vignette darkening the corners of the room. Preserve Video 1 exactly: 100% facial identity, facial features, skin texture, hairstyle, body proportions, clothing, expressions, gestures, movements, timing, camera position, framing, background, and environment. Do not alter, replace, or regenerate any physical elements. Naturally relight the original scene so the new lighting color, shadows, highlights, and exposure feel physically realistic and integrated with the existing room — like the same room under cold greenish fluorescent light. Match the mood without simply applying a green tint filter or preset. Keep the result photorealistic, consistent frame-to-frame, sharp, and natural. No face changes, beautification, artificial skin, warping, added objects, background changes, motion changes, or AI artifacts. ONLY change: lighting, exposure, color grading, contrast, shadows, highlights, atmosphere, and overall cinematic mood.

Notice what all three do: every sentence in the treatment names something
that is really in the frame (sky, concrete, skyline, window, wall, table,
floor, hoodie, beanie, sneakers, skin) and says what it becomes. Nothing is
added, nothing is replaced.
